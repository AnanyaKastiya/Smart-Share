### Slide 5: Strategic Trade-Off: Zero-CapEx Behavioral Model vs. Hardware Trap
* **Slide Title:** Strategic Trade-Off: Zero-CapEx Behavioral Model vs. Hardware Trap
* **Category Tag:** Business Operations & Feasibility
* **Visual Elements on Slide:**
  - Left Column: Executive Architectural Analysis contrasting hardware flaws vs. behavioral software superiority.
  - Right Column: **`diagram_zerocapex_arch.png`** (The IoT Hardware Trap Rejected vs. Zero-CapEx Mechanism Delivered).
  - Metrics Callout: ₹40,000 CapEx & fragile maintenance vs. ₹0 Hardware Spend & 100% uptime.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > "When evaluating solutions, the default engineering instinct is often: 'Let's install smart plugs, solenoid door locks, and CCTV cameras.' I deliberately rejected this hardware approach based on three business realities: First, commercial IoT costs ₹20,000 to ₹40,000 per facility. Second, building landlords strictly forbid third parties from tampering with main electrical switchboards. Third, basement Wi-Fi dead zones cause hardware to disconnect, creating downtime.
  > Instead, SmartShare implements a 100% Zero-CapEx software model. We use laminated 4-digit PINs taped to assets, mathematical buffer geometry, and an automated Rent Demerit Ledger. In residential facilities, appending a ₹150 fine directly to a tenant's monthly rent invoice produces far higher compliance than a fragile smart plug."
