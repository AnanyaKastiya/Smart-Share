## 📌 Executive Summary

**SmartShare** is a self-governing resource allocation and scheduling platform designed for high-density co-living, PG accommodations, and residential communities. 

Shared amenities (washing machines, sports turfs, study pods) inevitably suffer from the **"Tragedy of the Commons"**:
* **Peak Clumping:** 80% of demand clusters into narrow 12-hour weekend windows while mid-week capacity sits idle at 12%.
* **Physical Checking Tax:** Tenants walk downstairs every 45 minutes to check availability, only to find uncollected items abandoned in drums.
* **Zero Accountability:** Walk-in poachers steal slots, roommate cartels monopolize assets, and equipment damages have no audit trail.

**The SmartShare Solution:** Instead of spending ₹40,000+ per facility on fragile IoT relays and solenoids, SmartShare implements a **100% Zero-CapEx behavioral mechanism** that aligns human incentives with mathematical buffer geometry, digital custody passes, and direct rent-invoice financial demerits.

## ⚙️ Core Behavioral Mechanisms (Zero-CapEx)

1. **Persistent 15-Credit Weekly Quota:**
   - Every resident receives 15 credits weekly. Peak slots cost more; off-peak slots cost less. Credits deduct persistently across logins.
2. **48-Hour Rolling Reservation Window:**
   - Slots open strictly 48 hours in advance, completely stopping users from hoarding prime weekend slots weeks ahead.
3. **Dynamic Buffer Geometry:**
   - 10–15 minute mathematical buffers absorb cycle variations (e.g. laundry spin delay) without delaying subsequent users.
4. **Active Digital Custody Pass (Social Proof):**
   - An on-screen glowing green badge proves legal booking custody, allowing tenants to evict walk-in poachers instantly without arguing.
5. **Hamper Clearance Bounty Protocol (+2 Credits):**
   - If a previous user leaves clothes in a drum, the incoming user unloads them into the owner\'s basket and enters the 4-digit PIN taped to the hamper rack, earning **+2 credits** funded by the offender.
6. **Direct Rent Demerit Meter (₹10–15/min fine):**
   - For sports turfs and meeting rooms, overstay fees are auto-appended directly to monthly PG rent invoices, ensuring 100% compliance.
7. **Early Release Rebate (50% Refund):**
   - Finishing early and releasing a slot gives a 50% credit refund, instantly freeing capacity for community peers.
